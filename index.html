Cosmic Inflation & The Big Bang

Proposed by Alan Guth and Andrei Linde, cosmic inflation posits an ultra-rapid exponential expansion of spacetime driven by an inflaton scalar field. As inflation ended, reheating populated the cosmos with hot quark-gluon plasma. When matter and radiation decoupled 380,000 years later, the first light broke free, immortalized today as the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB).

ACOUSTIC PARALLEL: Acoustic detonation. An explosive sub-drop sweeps frequencies open as the kick drum ignites. Rising arpeggiated frequency debris mimics photons escaping the primordial fog, as user clicks send radial shockwaves across the coordinate matrix.
